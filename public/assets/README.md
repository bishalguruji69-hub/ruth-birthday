# 📁 Drop your media here

This is where you add your real photos and video for Ruth's birthday site.

## 📸 Photos — put in `photos/`

| File | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| `hero-ruth.jpg` | The main photo hanging in the heart frame on the tree (Stage 3) — pick your favorite of her |
| `letter-photo.jpg` | Photo on the left page of the love letter (Stage 6) — a photo of you two together is perfect |
| `gallery-1.jpg` → `gallery-5.jpg` | The photo carousel at the end (Stage 7) — any 1-5 photos you love |

## 🎬 Video — put in `video/`

| File | Notes |
|---|---|
| `birthday-video.mp4` | Your recorded video message. MP4 format, ideally under ~50 MB so it loads fast on her phone. |

## 🎵 Music (optional) — put in `audio/`

| File | Notes |
|---|---|
| `bg-music.mp3` | A soft instrumental track. Plays in the background after she unlocks the site. |

---

## ✏️ Notes

- Filenames are case-sensitive — use lowercase exactly as shown above.
- The site works **immediately** even without these files (placeholder images are shown).
- Drop your real files in **with the exact same names** and the site picks them up automatically.
- For best results: photos ~1200px wide, JPG format, compressed for web (TinyPNG / Squoosh).
- For the video: keep it under 50 MB; use HandBrake or similar if you need to compress.
